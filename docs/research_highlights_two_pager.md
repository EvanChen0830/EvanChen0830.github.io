# Research Methodology Highlights — Evan Chen

*Feature selection, label construction, alpha evaluation, and generative (diffusion) modeling for cross-sectional return prediction.*

The thread running through all four areas below is the same: **separate what was learned from what gets to grade it.** Every number reported — a feature's quality, an alpha's significance, a model's claimed edge — is computed against a null distribution generated by the same procedure, and every selection decision is made on a discovery split that never touches the data used to evaluate it. The specific techniques differ by stage; the discipline doesn't.

## Label Construction: Building a Coordinate System for "Predictive Power"

Before any feature is scored, the target itself is restructured so that "predictive power" has one precise, decomposable meaning. Raw forward returns are **within-date demeaned** to strip the market-wide component — a day-level constant cancels automatically in any cross-sectional statistic once the feature side is demeaned, which is the basis for every null-contamination guarantee downstream. Returns are grouped into **horizon bands** (non-overlapping or deliberately incremental, chosen where a label's autocorrelation structure actually changes) and **whitened** against their own shrinkage covariance, so every later statistic is computed in a rank-controlled, unit-scaled space rather than against a raw, numerically dominated target.

The central construction is an **orthogonal lift of the whitened return into three economically distinct blocks**: a Direction block (the return itself — who outperforms), an Activity block (the outer-product residual — dispersion and co-movement structure, independent of which side wins), and a Tail block (direction reweighted by its own magnitude — conditional, large-move-only predictive power). These three blocks are built to be exactly orthogonal under the cross-sectional weighting, so a feature's or alpha's entire relationship to the target decomposes additively and without leakage across them. In parallel, multiple **label families** (raw return, cross-sectionally demeaned, risk-adjusted, PCA-factor residual, time-series-detrended) expose different transformations of the same underlying outcome to a model without ever changing what "success" means for the evaluation metric that is ultimately reported.

## Feature Selection: A Geometric, Null-Calibrated Procedure, Not a Leaderboard

The predictive relationship between a feature library and the lifted target is a single cross-covariance operator — literally a matrix of information coefficients in a fixed coordinate system, decomposable day-by-day into a pure cross-sectional statistic pooled over time. Its **significant rank** is estimated by permutation parallel analysis using block-circular date shifts (never splitting a trading day), comparing the *j*-th observed singular value against the null's *j*-th — not its first — which is what correctly accounts for already-accepted modes when testing for more. Because the same selection procedure that chooses features also tends to flatter them if allowed to self-grade, every eligibility and selection decision is made on a **discovery split**, with final quality reported only on a disjoint evaluation window.

A feature's quality score splits additively across the Direction/Activity/Tail decomposition ($Q = Q_D + Q_V + Q_T$), which prevents the single most common feature-research error: crediting a feature with "alpha" when its entire predictive content is actually volatility-timing or tail-conditional information that a directional long-short book cannot use. Redundancy is handled in two tiers — mechanism-similarity clustering (which features answer the same economic question) nested with raw correlation families (which features are numerically the same bet) — with a closed-form family-representative rule and a **submodular greedy coverage objective** that comes with a certified approximation guarantee rather than an ad hoc heuristic. A **direction-first budget allocation** protects the channel a long-short portfolio actually depends on from being diluted by a fairness mechanism designed to protect the other two channels, and a soft, tradable-universe-scaled discount — not a hard cutoff — down-weights features whose signal is concentrated in a handful of names (via an inverse-Herfindahl effective-sample-size diagnostic) without unilaterally vetoing a genuinely strong concentrated feature.

## Alpha Evaluation: Full, Inherited, and Novel

The same discipline is re-applied one layer up, to a trained model's output rather than to raw features. An alpha is evaluated under a **full / inherited / novel representation convention**: how much of its predictive content is already explainable by the feature library it was built from (inherited), and how much is genuinely new information the model discovered (novel) — a distinction a single IC number cannot make. Before crediting a model's nonlinearity with anything, a **walk-forward linear surrogate** is fit on the same selected features; only the **residual alpha** — what the linear surrogate fails to reconstruct — is eligible to be called the model's own contribution. Every research run is resolved into one of a small number of **named diagnostic outcomes** (e.g., genuine novel signal, fully inherited, mechanism drift, overfit to noise) rather than left as a single ambiguous performance metric, so a result is always actionable rather than merely reported.

## Generative Modeling: From a Point Forecast to the Full Conditional Distribution

The most structurally different piece of the methodology replaces the point-prediction paradigm entirely: instead of estimating $\mathbb{E}[Y \mid X]$, a **diffusion model learns the full joint conditional return distribution** $p(Y \mid X)$ across tickers and horizons simultaneously. Training follows the score-based formulation — a forward process adds noise on a schedule, and the network is trained to recover the added noise, which is itself an estimate of the score function $\nabla_x \log p(x)$ used to run the reverse-time generative process. Classifier-free and auto-guidance control how strongly the conditioning signal shapes the sampled distribution at inference time.

Evaluating a distributional forecast is itself a nontrivial problem, since only one realized outcome is ever observed per signal, never the distribution it came from. The resolution is a **proxy-distribution test**: pool realized outcomes from other signals whose *predicted* distributions are close to a given signal's predicted distribution, and compare that pooled empirical distribution against the prediction directly — turning calibration into a falsifiable, repeatable test rather than an article of faith. This framing also **subsumes the Direction/Activity/Tail decomposition** rather than sitting beside it: a learned distribution's first moment is the direction signal, its dispersion and co-movement structure is the activity signal, and its tail mass is the tail signal — three diagnostics that are normally bolted onto a point forecast become three different slices of one object that can be inspected directly. Live applications of this framing include multi-horizon market-making regime selection (does the short-horizon predicted distribution agree or conflict with the long-horizon one) and cross-ticker co-movement modeling via jointly predicted label vectors.

## The Common Thread

Four different technical problems — reshaping a target, selecting among thousands of candidate features, judging a model's output, and learning a full return distribution instead of a point forecast — are held to the same three standards throughout: a discovery/evaluation boundary that is never crossed, significance measured against a null built from the exact procedure being tested rather than a generic table, and an explicit accounting of *what kind* of predictive power a number represents before asking how large it is. The architecture changes by stage; the standard of evidence does not.
