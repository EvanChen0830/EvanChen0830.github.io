## Research Interests

- **Deep Generative Modeling in Finance**: Learning joint conditional return distributions $p(Y \mid X)$ across multi-asset, multi-horizon panels using Transformer denoisers, classifier-free guidance, and fast diffusion samplers.
- **Cross-Sectional Predictive Geometry**: Decomposing predictive structure into mutually orthogonal Direction ($\Phi_{\mathsf D}$), Activity ($\Phi_{\mathsf V}$), and Tail ($\Phi_{\mathsf T}$) channels under rigorous permutation null baselines.
- **Feature Selection & Submodular Coverage**: Independence-aware eligibility filtering and certified submodular greedy coverage fill to protect directional trading exposure.
- **Alpha Evaluation & Residual Signal**: Walk-forward linear surrogates and residual alpha decomposition to verify whether complex non-linear models generate genuinely novel trading signals beyond known feature exposures.
