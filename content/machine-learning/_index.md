This section collects notes, implementation guides, and experiments in modern machine learning — focusing on generative modeling, probabilistic deep learning, and architectural design principles.

## Research Themes

- **Deep Generative Models & Diffusion**  
  Continuous and discrete diffusion models, score-based generative modeling, classifier-free guidance, noise scheduling, fast ODE/SDE samplers, and likelihood evaluation.
  
- **Sequence Modeling & Transformers**  
  Attention mechanisms, tokenization design for structured and multi-dimensional panel data, cross-attention gating, and architectural scaling.

- **Probabilistic Inference & Calibration**  
  Evaluating full predictive distributions $p(Y \mid X)$ beyond point predictions: continuous ranked probability score (CRPS), energy scores, binned log-likelihoods, and quantile calibration.

- **Representation Geometry**  
  Latent space disentanglement, orthogonal mechanism projections, invariance under affine transforms, and out-of-distribution robustness.
